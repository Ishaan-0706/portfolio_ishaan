On 28 September, the last day of registration, we put together our pitch video for the BitMela Ideathon and submitted it. We hoped we would get a chance to take the idea further.

We ended up finishing among the top three out of 1,400 entries. Between those two moments were an interview we thought we had messed up, four weeks of demanding mentorship, and a final pitch in Jaipur.

## An interview we thought had gone wrong

Making the top 20 was our first big moment. Next came an interview with Santosh Vadivelu and Palash Agrawal. We walked out thinking we had messed it up.

Then we found out we had made the final six.

That meant four weeks of mentorship with Santosh, Palash, and Freddie New. Each week brought difficult questions, honest criticism, and feedback that pushed us to rethink what we were building.

## Google for AI agents

Our idea was something we described as “Google for AI agents.”

As the AI agent ecosystem grows, finding the right agent for a particular task becomes harder. Discovery is only part of the problem: once you find an agent, how do you judge whether it is capable, reliable, and worth using?

We envisioned a platform where people could discover agents based on their requirements, compare their capabilities and performance, and use verification and rankings to make more informed choices.

The goal was to make AI agents easier to discover, evaluate, and trust. These were the capabilities we envisioned for the platform, rather than a claim that we had already solved every part of that problem.

## Learning to question our own idea

The most valuable part of the mentorship was having to reconsider an idea we were already attached to.

It is easy to explain why you believe in something. It is harder to sit with criticism, question your assumptions, and be willing to take the idea apart and rebuild it.

Our mentors kept challenging our thinking. Those conversations became one of the most memorable parts of the experience, and a lesson I want to carry into the next thing I build.

## The final in Jaipur

On 30 October, we were in Jaipur for BitMela.

Udayrajsinh Vala and Hasit Chandra Koppuravuri delivered the final pitch, and we finished among the top three. Going from a last-day submission to that stage was a moment our team will remember for a long time.

Thank you to the judges—Freddie New, Kunal K Kapoor, Adi Shankara, and Alexander Mann—for hearing our idea, challenging our thinking, and sharing their perspectives.

## The people behind the journey

Akhil Dhyani played an essential role in helping us reach that stage. I’m grateful to Santosh Vadivelu, Palash Agrawal, and Freddie New for their demanding and invaluable mentorship, and to Devlup Labs for supporting us throughout the journey.

Thank you also to Aryan Jain, Kushagra Kinra, Khushvendra Singh, and Aradhya Mahajan for helping us along the way.

And to my teammates—Rishabh Raj, Udayrajsinh Vala, Tanay Bhatt, and Hasit Chandra Koppuravuri—thank you for sharing every uncertain step and every exciting result.

We came in with an idea and an ambition. We left with a trophy, new connections, memories, and a better understanding of how much an idea can change when you let other people challenge it.

That is the lesson I want to take into what we build next.
